What is inside

What the watch measures, shown as it measured it.

Reads the watch Heart rate, blood oxygen, activity and motion come from the Veyro watch over Bluetooth. Figures the watch does not report are left blank rather than estimated.
Keeps it local Readings are stored on your device. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded to run the app.
Emergency help An SOS you can dial with one press, using an emergency number you choose, and fall detection you can switch off.
Honest by design A wellness tool. It does not diagnose, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

Requirements

PlatformNeeds
WindowsWindows 10 or newer, 64-bit, Bluetooth

The watch is read over Bluetooth Low Energy. Digital Saver runs entirely on your machine; the only network calls it makes are checking for updates and, if you choose to use the assistant, the questions you send it.

Verify your download

Checksums are published next to every artifact.

Windows

Run this in PowerShell next to the downloaded files.

Get-FileHash .\windows.zip -Algorithm SHA256

Compare the output with windows.zip.sha256.

Linux

Verify the archive you downloaded.

sha256sum -c linux.tar.gz.sha256

A result of OK means the file is intact.
